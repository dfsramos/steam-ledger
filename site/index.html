Steam Ledger

Track what you paid for your Steam game skins, what they're worth now, and your profit and loss over time — entirely on your own machine.

Download for Windows
Free and open source · View on GitHub
Features

What it does

Portfolio dashboard

Every item you own, current market value, P/L per item and overall — sortable and filterable by game.

Real Steam import

Pulls your actual Community Market history and current inventory, reconciles the two, and fills in prices from Store purchases and pack receipts.

One-click account connect

A real Steam login window opens inside the app and picks up your session automatically — no digging through browser devtools.

Live market prices

Refresh current values from the Steam Community Market on demand, per item or for your whole portfolio.

Privacy

Local-first, by design

Nothing leaves your machine except requests to Steam itself.

  • Your portfolio is a SQLite file at ~/.steamledger/vault.db — no account, no cloud sync, no telemetry.
  • Your Steam session cookie is stored via your OS's native credential store (Windows Credential Manager), never in the app's database or a plain file.
  • It's only ever sent directly to Steam's own servers, the same way your browser already does.
Get started

Building from source

Prerequisites: Node.js 18+, Rust, and the Tauri v2 prerequisites for your OS.

git clone https://github.com/dfsramos/steam-ledger.git
cd steam-ledger
npm install
npm run tauri dev
