01 — WHAT IT DOES

Four things, done properly.

No portfolio "sync service", no browser extension, no pasting cookies into a text box.

01

Portfolio dashboard

Every item with its cost basis, current market value, and profit or loss — per item and across the whole vault.

sortable · per-game filters · realized vs unrealized
02

Real purchase history

Imports Community Market history, in-game Store purchases, and multi-item pack receipts, splitting pack cost across its contents.

reconciled against your live inventory,
so resold items don't linger in the books
03

One-click Steam connect

Opens a genuine Steam login window inside the app. You sign in to Steam, not to us. No manual cookie copying, no password ever touching the app.

session handled by the OS, not by a config file
04

On-demand price refresh

Pull current Market prices when you ask for it — not on a timer, not in the background, never hammering Steam's endpoints.

you press refresh · nothing polls behind your back
✓
SAFE TO USE

The full source is open, so you can read exactly what happens with your Steam session before you trust it with a cookie. That cookie is used for exactly one thing: reading your own Community Market history, Store purchase history, and inventory directly from Steam's own endpoints. Nothing is bought, sold, traded, or changed on your account, and nothing is sent anywhere except Steam itself.

02 — WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES

It never leaves your machine.

Steam Ledger has no backend. There is no server to sign up for, nothing to sync, and nothing to leak. The only hosts it ever talks to are Steam's own, using your own session — the same requests your browser makes when you open your Market history.

All of it is auditable: the source is MIT-licensed and the network layer is a few hundred lines you can read in an afternoon.

DATABASE
~/.steamledger/vault.db (SQLite, plain file, yours to back up or delete)
SESSION COOKIE
Windows Credential Manager — OS-native encrypted storage, never written to disk in plaintext
NETWORK
steamcommunity.com, store.steampowered.com and help.steampowered.com only. No analytics host, no update ping, no third party.
ACCOUNT
None. There is nothing to register for.
TELEMETRY
None. Zero events, zero crash reporting, zero identifiers.
03 — FOR DEVELOPERS

Don't trust the installer? Build it.

Tauri + Rust backend, SQLite storage. Node 18+ and a Rust toolchain are the only prerequisites.

bash
$ git clone https://github.com/dfsramos/steam-ledger.git
$ cd steam-ledger
$ npm install
$ npm run tauri dev▍

Know what your inventory actually cost you.
